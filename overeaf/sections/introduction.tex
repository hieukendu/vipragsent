\section{Introduction}

Sentiment analysis is often posed as polarity classification, although pragmatic meaning can reverse or qualify the reading suggested by surface wording. In Vietnamese social-media comments, implicit sentiment, sarcasm, irony, idiom/figurative language, code-switching, and mocking are distinct phenomena that can co-occur with, but are not reducible to, polarity or emotion. We model the six phenomena as binary tasks and retain polarity and emotion as auxiliary signals rather than treating all labels as one emotion taxonomy.

Vietnamese encoders provide complementary starting points: PhoBERT is a Vietnamese-specific pretrained model \citep{nguyen-tuan-nguyen-2020-phobert}, whereas XLM-R supports multilingual transfer \citep{conneau-etal-2020-unsupervised} and ViSoBERT adapts the XLM-R family to Vietnamese social media \citep{nguyen-etal-2023-visobert}. We study whether a shared XLM-R-large encoder can support this bundle of pragmatic tasks while preserving separate prediction interfaces for pragmatic labels, polarity, and emotion.

Our contributions are threefold. First, we formulate Vietnamese pragmatic sentiment as six potentially co-occurring binary phenomena: implicit sentiment, sarcasm, irony, idiom/figurative language, code-switching, and mocking, while retaining polarity and emotion as auxiliary affective labels, and introduce ViPragSent, an 11,997-comment Vietnamese social-media dataset annotated under this formulation. Second, we develop an XLM-R-large multi-task model that jointly predicts the six pragmatic labels while using polarity, emotion, and training-only rationale supervision as auxiliary objectives. Third, we provide a controlled empirical study spanning in-domain pragmatic classification, external affective transfer, component and computational trade-offs, positive-label scarcity, calibration, and error behavior. The proposed model reaches $93.67 \pm 0.19$ macro-pragmatic F1 and has the highest observed mean on all six pragmatic heads among the evaluated systems with complete three-run results.
