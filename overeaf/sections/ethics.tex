\section{Ethical Considerations}

Pragmatic labels are context-dependent and may be interpreted differently by readers. Errors can mischaracterize intent, emotion, or attitude, especially for culturally specific, offensive, personal, or ambiguous text. The system should support human review rather than autonomous or high-impact decisions. Aggregate metrics do not establish equal performance across demographic groups, domains, or writing styles, and the model is not intended to infer protected attributes.
