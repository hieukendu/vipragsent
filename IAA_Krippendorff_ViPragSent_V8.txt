Method
------
Metric: Krippendorff's alpha with nominal distance.
The calculation uses the repository's authoritative
krippendorff_alpha_nominal implementation and reads the three CSV files
directly from the ZIP archive.

The primary IAA is the agreement between the two independent human
annotators (Annotator 1 vs Annotator 2). The gold file is an adjudicated
reference, not an independent annotator, so it is reported separately as a
three-source supplementary analysis rather than silently mixing it into the
primary IAA.

Fields: implicit_sentiment, sarcasm, irony, idiom_figurative,
        code_switching, mocking, polarity, emotion.
