## Safety and reproducibility boundary

- No model was downloaded, loaded, trained, or run.
- The calculation uses recorded prediction JSONL files for seeds 20260521,
  20260522, and 20260523 under `reports/q1a_matched_xlmr_ft/` and its
  `proposed_source/` subdirectory.
- Each seed has 2,000 paired test rows with matching sample IDs and matching
  six-label gold tuples between the two systems.
- This is a deterministic calculation over stored artifacts, not a fresh
  training or inference reproducibility rerun.

## Method

- Statistic: binary macro-F1 for each of the six pragmatic labels, plus the
  unweighted macro-pragmatic F1 across the six labels.
- Difference: proposed (`ours`) minus matched baseline, in percentage points
  (pp).
- Resampling: 10,000 paired hierarchical bootstrap replicates; first resample
  the three seeds, then resample the 2,000 paired test examples within each
  selected seed.
- Bootstrap random seed: 20260525.
- Interval: percentile 95% CI.
- `crosses zero` is inclusive. The raw p-value is the paired sign-based
  two-sided p-value from the locked analysis implementation.

## Interpretation

- The nominal macro-pragmatic 95% bootstrap CI is entirely above zero.
- All six individual-head CIs cross zero. The stored data therefore support
  observed positive head gains, but not a zero-crossing-free claim for any
  individual head under this interval calculation.
- The raw macro sign p is approximately 0.0290. With seven metrics, the
  rank-one Holm adjustment is approximately 0.2030; do not claim family-wise
  significance after Holm correction.
- The evidence-bounded summary is: the observed three-seed macro gap is
  +0.63 pp and its nominal paired-bootstrap 95% CI is [+0.07, +1.20] pp;
  individual head gains remain uncertain because their CIs cross zero.
