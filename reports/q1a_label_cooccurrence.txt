## Definition

For each row, k is the number of positive labels among:

implicit_sentiment, sarcasm, irony, idiom_figurative, code_switching, mocking

P(k=0), P(k=1), and P(k>=2) use all rows in the stated scope as the
denominator. Pair percentages are also pair count divided by all rows; they are
not conditional probabilities given one label.

## Interpretation

- Across all V8 rows, 37.8178% have at least one pragmatic label and 17.3793%
  have at least two.
- The Q1a test split has fewer zero-label rows than full V8 (57.90% versus
  62.18%) and more exactly-one-label rows (26.25% versus 20.44%).
- Implicit sentiment + mocking is the most frequent pair in both scopes.
