## Safety and reproducibility boundary

- No model was downloaded, loaded, trained, or run.
- The comparison uses stored prediction JSONL artifacts for seeds 20260521,
  20260522, and 20260523, with 2,000 paired test rows per seed: 6,000
  paired rows in total.
- The matched baseline is the new six-head XLM-R-large artifact. `ours` is
  the existing proposed Q1a prediction artifact. The two systems are paired
  on the same sample IDs and the same six-label gold tuples.
- Counts below pool the three seeds. The same test sample can therefore appear
  once per seed; these are pooled prediction records, not 6,000 unique texts.

## Definition

For each head, `ours_correct_matched_wrong` means that the proposed prediction
equals gold while the matched-baseline prediction does not. The reverse count
means that the matched baseline is correct while the proposed prediction is
wrong. The F1 table reports the arithmetic mean of the three recorded seed F1
values; the macro row is the unweighted mean across the six pragmatic heads.

## Limits of semantic error categories

The artifacts support exact counts by target label, prediction direction,
confusion matrix, and multi-label correction. They do not contain a manual
category field for semantic claims such as surface-positive/intended-negative,
target-directed ridicule, or lexical polarity mismatch. Such categories
should not be assigned exact counts without a separate human annotation pass.

No causal claim is made that rationale supervision or any other training choice
caused the observed differences; this report only describes stored predictions
and gold labels.
