# ADR-Q4-CALIBRATION-CHECKPOINT-CONFLICT

## Status

CONFLICT, awaiting an explicit scientific protocol decision.

## Decision

The Q4 paper labels `phobert_finetune` and `vistral_7b_sft` resolve to pragmatic-only
checkpoints under the locked role registry. Those checkpoints do not expose the required
three-way intended-polarity probability vector. The implementation must not substitute a
multitask checkpoint or fabricate a polarity head.

The full Vistral ViPragSent checkpoint is resolved because it owns a polarity head.

## Consequence

Q4 calibration export and the Phase 14/14.5 readiness gate remain BLOCKED until the
scientific mapping is explicitly approved. No Q4 results are generated by setup or fixture runs.
